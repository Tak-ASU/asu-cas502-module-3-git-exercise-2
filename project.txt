At Liberty Life, I spearheaded a project to optimize a critical life insurance underwriting process that was far too complex for traditional computational methods. The process involved so many variables that the possible permutations exceeded a googol (10^100), making it impossible to search through conventional means. To solve this, I engineered a custom genetic algorithm from scratch in Python, encoding the business process into virtual chromosomes. By simulating evolutionary mechanics—allowing populations of solutions to "breed" and exchange genetic material—the algorithm evolved increasingly efficient outcomes over multiple generations. This robust, well-thought-out solution allowed me to decode the "DNA" of the best-performing offspring into actionable business optimizations that balanced cost, time, and risk.
