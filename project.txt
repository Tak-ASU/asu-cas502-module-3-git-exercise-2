April 2017 – July 2018: Process Optimization Using Genetic AlgorithmsDuring this period at Liberty Life, I spearheaded a project to optimize a critical life insurance underwriting process that was far too complex for traditional computational methods. The process involved so many variables that the possible permutations exceeded a googol ($10^{100}$), making it impossible to search through conventional means. To solve this, I engineered a custom genetic algorithm from scratch in Python, encoding the business process into virtual chromosomes. By simulating evolutionary mechanics—allowing populations of solutions to "breed" and exchange genetic material—the algorithm evolved increasingly efficient outcomes over multiple generations. This robust solution allowed me to decode the "DNA" of the best-performing offspring into actionable business optimizations.
Technical Skills: Python, Numpy, Pandas, Genetic Algorithms, VBA, MS SQL Server.

November 2021 – August 2024: Predictive Maintenance for Mining Operations
While at First Quantum Minerals, I led the creation of machine learning models to monitor mining activities and inform maintenance decisions. Operating in a harsh environment where a fleet of approximately 100 trucks streams 2,000,000 sensor readings daily, I utilized machine learning to extract anomalies from 21 different engine signals. Because engine failures were not always explicitly recorded, I deployed anomaly detection to rank the most severe issues and send alerts to the maintenance team. This system enabled the team to focus their interventions on specific high-risk trucks, improving fleet reliability and researching novel data science techniques for a diverse project portfolio. Technical Skills: Python, Microsoft Azure, SQL, Anomaly Detection, Supervised Machine Learning.
